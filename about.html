Hi, I'm Deogyne

Machine Learning Analyst Student | Student Councillor, SANQC | Board Director, ASEC | Community Advocate

I'm driven by curiosity, continuous learning and the opportunity to use data, AI and technology to make a meaningful impact. My goal is to become a data scientist and use data and AI to solve real-world problems. I believe in challenging myself, stepping outside my comfort zone and achieving what I set my mind to.

I'm a second-year Machine Learning Analyst Diploma student at NorQuest College, building my skills in data analytics, machine learning and AI. I enjoy exploring data, identifying patterns and turning information into insights that can support better decisions. Through academic and work-integrated learning projects, I've worked with datasets containing over 243,000 Go Auto service appointments and environmental data for Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC).

My journey into technology has not been a straight line. My previous experience as a Health Care Aide, including working in rural Alberta hospitals, taught me adaptability, empathy, communication and how to remain calm under pressure. These experiences shaped how I approach challenges and strengthened my appreciation for understanding people's needs.

Beyond technology, I have actively sought opportunities to grow through leadership and community involvement. I serve as a Student Councillor with the Students' Association of NorQuest College (SANQC) and a Board Director with the Alberta Students' Executive Council (ASEC). These roles have challenged me to take responsibility, consider different perspectives and contribute to conversations that affect post-secondary students.

I'm also passionate about technology education and creating opportunities for young learners. Through volunteering with Black Boys Code and the Africans & African Descendants Friendship Club of St. Albert (AADFC), I've introduced children to coding, robotics, Python and artificial intelligence. I enjoy seeing their curiosity grow, their confidence develop and their excitement when they understand something new.

I look forward to continuing to learn, collaborate and contribute to meaningful work at the intersection of data, technology and society.
