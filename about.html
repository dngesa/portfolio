Hi, I'm Deogyne

Machine Learning Analyst Student | Student Councillor, SANQC | Board Director, ASEC | Community Advocate

I bring a combination of analytical thinking, curiosity, adaptability, communication and a willingness to learn. My experiences in healthcare, technology, teaching and student leadership have strengthened my ability to approach challenges from different perspectives, work collaboratively and take initiative. I am also able to break down complex tasks in a way anyone can understand.

I'm interested in using data and machine learning to solve practical problems that organizations and communities face. This includes identifying patterns and trends, understanding what drives outcomes, improving processes, supporting better decision-making and developing predictive solutions that help anticipate future challenges. I'm particularly interested in projects where data can turn complex problems into actionable insights.

I'm interested in opportunities as a Data Analyst or Machine Learning Analyst, where I can apply my analytical and technical skills to real-world challenges, continue developing my experience and contribute to meaningful projects. My long-term goal is to grow into a Data Scientist, combining data analysis, machine learning and AI to develop impactful solutions.

I am driven by curiosity, continuous learning and the opportunity to use data, AI and technology to make a meaningful impact. My goal is to become a data scientist and use data and AI to solve real-world problems. I believe in challenging myself and achieving what I set my mind to.

I am a second-year Machine Learning Analyst Diploma student at NorQuest College, building a foundation in data analytics, machine learning and AI. Through academic projects, I have developed skills in Python, SQL, data analysis, machine learning and data visualization. I enjoy exploring data, identifying patterns and turning information into insights. My projects include analyzing over 243,000 Go Auto service appointments and working with environmental data for Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC). I am working towards the IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate.

Beyond technology, I have made a conscious effort to step outside my comfort zone and grow through leadership and governance. I serve as a Student Councillor with the Students' Association of NorQuest College (SANQC), where I hold signing authority and sit on the Academic Council. I am also a Board Member of the Alberta Students' Executive Council (ASEC), contributing to the representation and advocacy of 133,000 post-secondary students across Alberta. These roles challenge me to learn, take responsibility, consider different perspectives and grow.

I am passionate about technology education and community involvement. As a STEM Instructor with Black Boys Code, I support children aged 8 - 17 in coding, robotics and STEM. I also volunteered with the Africans & African Descendants Friendship Club of St. Albert (AADFC), planning and delivering introductory AI and Python lessons for children aged 6 - 12. It is rewarding to watch their curiosity grow, see their persistence and share their excitement when they grasp new concepts.

My experience as a Health Care Aide has shaped me into an empathetic and adaptable professional. Working in rural Alberta hospitals, including Hanna, Smoky Lake and Drayton Valley, often in understaffed environments, taught me to adapt quickly, remain calm under pressure and work collaboratively. It strengthened my communication and problem-solving skills and taught me to understand individual needs and act with compassion.

I look forward to learning, collaborating and contributing to meaningful work at the intersection of data, technology and society.
